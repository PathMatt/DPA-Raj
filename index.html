Case PV26-001 · The diagnosis

CIC-rearranged sarcoma

Patchy CD99 and nuclear ETV4 and WT1 pointed the way in Columbus. DUX4 and fusion testing in Cincinnati settled it.

CD99

Patchy, not diffuse · Ohio State

ETV4 · WT1

Nuclear · Ohio State

DUX4 · Fusion

Cincinnati Children's · confirms it

It behaves more aggressively than Ewing sarcoma and responds less well to Ewing-type chemotherapy. Getting the name right changes what the oncologist does next.

Entity features: WHO Classification of Tumours, Soft Tissue and Bone Tumours, 5th ed. (2020)

What's next · in design

An intelligence layer institutions can shape

Configurable, not one-size-fits-all.

Every department works differently, and in this field any agent you freeze today is out of date in months.

We provide the infrastructure and keep it current. Each institution decides what it does, and an academic center or a private lab can set it up without an engineering team.
